_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

Shorts Bot is a personal tool that creates short videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

## What data it uses
- **YouTube:** permission to upload videos (`youtube.upload`) to the owner's own channel. It does not read, collect or store any other YouTube or Google account data.
- **Sign-in tokens** are stored privately as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and on the owner's own computer. They are never shared or sold.

## What it does not do
- It does not collect data from viewers or any other person.
- It does not use advertising trackers or cookies.
- It does not share data with third parties.

Shorts Bot's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access
The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
